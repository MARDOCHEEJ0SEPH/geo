## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Learn from others' errors:

**1. Waiting for Perfection**
Good content published beats perfect content delayed. Start with 80% and iterate to 100%.

**2. Copying SEO Tactics**
GEO isn't SEO 2.0. Different paradigm requires different approach.

**3. Technical-Only Focus**
Schema without quality content fails. Balance technical and content excellence.

**4. Impatience**
Most GEO results take 3-6 months. Consistency beats intensity.

**5. Ignoring Measurement**
You can't improve what you don't measure. Track systematically.

**6. Isolation**
GEO evolves rapidly. Connect with others, share learnings, stay current.
