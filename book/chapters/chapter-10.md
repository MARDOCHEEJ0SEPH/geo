- "Complete Guide to React Hooks"
- "React Performance Optimization: A Practical Guide"
- "Building Scalable React Applications"
- "React vs Vue vs Angular: Technical Comparison"

Each article featured:
- Answer-oriented structure
- Code examples
- Citation of official documentation
- FAQ sections
- HowTo schema markup

**Month 3-4: Authority Building**
- Conducted original research: "State of React Development 2024"
- Surveyed 300 developers
- Published comprehensive findings
- Created downloadable resources

**Month 5-6: Expansion**
- Published 12 additional articles
- Created topic cluster around React ecosystem
- Optimized existing content based on AI testing
- Built citation network with authoritative tech sites

**Results After 6 Months**:

**AI Visibility**:
- Cited in 67% of tested queries related to React development
- Primary source citation in 34% of responses
- Featured in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity responses

**Business Impact**:
- Inbound inquiries increased from 2-3 per month to 15-20
- Average project value increased from $8K to $18K
- Closed rate improved from 30% to 55%
- Revenue increased 340% year-over-year

**Quote**:
"When potential clients tell me they found me through ChatGPT recommending my React guide, I know GEO is working. AI has become my best sales agent."

**Key Lessons**:
1. Niche expertise beats broad coverage
2. Original research creates citation-worthy content
3. Comprehensive guides establish authority
4. Technical accuracy matters for developer content

## Lessons Learned Across All Cases

### What Works Universally

**1. Specificity Beats Breadth**
Every successful case focused on specific niches rather than broad categories.

**2. Original Research Creates Citations**
Cases with original research saw 2-3x higher citation rates.

**3. Comprehensive Coverage Matters**
Shallow content rarely gets cited; depth creates authority.

**4. Technical Implementation Is Table Stakes**
Schema, structure, and markup aren't optional.

**5. Consistency Compounds**
Long-term, consistent effort yields exponential results.

### Common Pitfalls

**1. Impatience**
Most cases took 3-6 months to see significant results.

**2. Keyword Thinking**
Approaching GEO like SEO consistently underperformed.

**3. Technical-Only Focus**
Schema without quality content didn't drive citations.

**4. Insufficient Depth**
Thin content rarely achieved visibility.

**5. Ignoring Updates**
Outdated content saw declining citation rates.

## Actionable Takeaways
